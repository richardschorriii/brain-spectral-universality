# Paper 02 — Universal Spectral Architecture of Resting-State Brain Networks

## Full Title
Universal Spectral Architecture of Resting-State Brain Networks: λ₂ Rescaling Reveals Scale-Invariant Eigenspectrum Topology Independent of Connectivity Method, Recording Modality, and Acquisition Site

## Key Results
- **Sites validated:** 17/17 ABIDE sites exceed r > 0.95 threshold
- **Grand mean:** r = 0.9851 spectral universality
- **Topology independence:** Consistent across connectivity methods, recording modalities, and acquisition sites
- **Rescaling:** λ₂ rescaling is the optimal parameter (ranked #1 across all comparisons)
